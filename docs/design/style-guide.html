Qory console · design direction v1

Quiet surfaces,
one drop of honey.

The console is where a small engineering team sees every run its coding agents make. It should feel like a well-kept instrument: dense but calm, warm but not sweet, and honest about what is there and what is not there yet.

01 Identity

A hexagon that reads as a Q

The mark is one cell of comb with its counter cut out and a tail crossing the lower-right wall. At 16 px it is a hexagon; at 22 px and up it is a Q. The wordmark is plain “Qory Apiary” in Geist Semibold, tightly tracked, never in capitals, never broken over two lines. “Qory” alone names the whole of Qory, runner included, and appears only in the trust sentence.

02 Colour

Warm neutrals, honey for the one thing that matters

Every grey leans 65 to 85° toward the accent, so the honey never looks pasted on. Primary marks the single main action on a screen and nothing else; the darker accent carries links and active icons where honey would fail contrast. Values are read live from the tokens.

qory · light

04 Foundations

Space, radius, elevation, motion

A 4 px grid. Three radii with three jobs. Shadows are reserved for things that float; everything resting on the page is drawn with a one-pixel border.
